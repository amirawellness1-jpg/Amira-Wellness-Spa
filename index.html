About Amira Wellness Spa

Welcome to Amira Wellness Spa, your destination for relaxation and wellness in Sector 1, Greater Noida. We aim to provide a peaceful and comfortable environment where guests can relax, refresh and enjoy a premium spa experience.

Our spa is located at Permount City Square, Permount Emotions, Sector 1, Greater Noida. Contact us to learn more about our available massage and wellness services and to book your appointment.
